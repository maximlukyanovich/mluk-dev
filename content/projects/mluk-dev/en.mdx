## Task

A personal site to write in two languages, keep the CV current and show the work — without a CMS or a database, and cheap to keep running.

## Solution

Next.js 16 with Cache Components. All content lives in git: posts in MDX, the CV and the projects in JSON, one file per locale. No i18n library — two dictionaries and `next/root-params`.

- Every page is prerendered in both languages.
- The design kit's CSS is used as it is, with Tailwind 4 utilities on top.
- The CV PDF is printed from the same JSON as the page.

## Result

Writing, Projects, CV and Contact in English and Russian. One source for every piece of content: a change to the CV reaches the page, its print sheet and the PDF in one commit.
